## What’s New in v0.6.0

LioraLang 0.6 brings an assistant that fills in your cards, a rebuilt deck editor and deck page, and the account and Hub tools a public app needs.

### Added

- Card suggestions: type a word and the assistant offers the rest of the card — the translation, the extra language, CEFR level, part of speech, two natural examples and tags. Suggestions appear in the empty fields; Tab takes them all, and nothing you typed is ever replaced. It works from either side, so you can start with the translation
- Fill in a pasted list: one click adds translations, levels, parts of speech, examples and tags to every line, and offers a fix for a misspelt word
- Collect a deck on a topic: give a topic, a level and how many words, look the words over and add them. Find it under New deck → Collect a deck with AI
- A hint after Again: when you miss a card in Learn, a short note on how to remember the word appears on the card until your next grade
- Picture decks in the Hub: decks with a picture side can be published, and show as Picture → Polish
- Delete account, in Account → Security: removes your account, synced decks and progress, and the decks you published. Decks on your device stay
- Report a Hub deck: a flag on every Hub deck page. A deck reported by several people is hidden until it is checked

The assistant needs a signed-in account and an internet connection, and can be turned off in Settings → New words. It runs on Google Gemini: what you type and your deck’s languages are sent to it.

### Improved

- The deck editor is built around adding words: one row that is always there, autosave, sides shown as the front and back of a card, and editing a word in place
- The deck page shows where you stand with the deck: what is learned, what is due and what to do next
- Session settings in Learn use the same calm controls as the rest of Settings
- The extra language has its own column in word lists and sits next to the two sides in the editor
- “Learn” is called Learn everywhere

### Fixed

- Words whose first side is a picture are added from quick add again
- Pictures upload to your account and sync between devices
- Starting with the translation in Add words no longer jumps the cursor back to the first field
- Session settings fit on a phone screen
- Settings scroll their sections and their list separately
- Sticker labels are no longer cut off

### Known issues

- The builds are not code-signed yet. Windows may show Unknown Publisher; on macOS, open the app the first time with right-click → Open.
- On macOS the update cannot install itself while the app is unsigned. Download the new version from this page.
